# Fashion Cymbal Ontology

Business knowledge bundle for Fashion Cymbal analytics.

## Metrics
- [[return-rate]] — ratio of returned items to items sold
- [[sell-through-rate]] — ratio of units sold to units received
- [[average-basket-value]] — mean order value across channels
- [[gross-margin]] — revenue minus cost of goods sold as percentage

## Entities
- [[product-category]] — hierarchical product classification (Department > Category > Subcategory)
- [[brand]] — brand identity and tier classification
- [[sales-channel]] — online, store, outlet, marketplace channels
- [[collection-season]] — seasonal collection lifecycle (Pre-Season, In-Season, Markdown, Outlet)

## Rules
- [[return-window-policy]] — channel-specific return period and exclusion logic
- [[markdown-cadence]] — seasonal price reduction schedule and thresholds
- [[outlet-vs-fullprice]] — classification rules separating outlet from full-price transactions
